# Privacy Policy

Version: 25 November 2026. Lean Playground is published by Serhii Ziborov. Contact: sergii.ziborov@gmail.com.

## In short

Lean Playground has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Lean runs on your device. We do not receive your files, your proofs or your progress.

## What stays on your device

- Your Lean files, lesson attempts and the results of their last check.
- Your progress in lessons, flashcards and labs.
- The course packs you download.
- Settings such as the code font size, line wrapping and recent searches.
- How long Lean takes to start on your device, used to show an estimate while it prepares.

This lives in the app's storage on your device and in your device backups, if you make them. The app does not send it anywhere. Deleting the app deletes it from the device.

## Network

The app goes online only to fetch the course catalog and the course packs you choose to download. They are public files on GitHub. The requests carry no account, no identifier and none of your data. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic request details when it serves the files; GitHub's privacy statement applies to that: <https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement>. Everything else works offline.

## Files you share

When you import, export or share a file, the app passes it only to the place you choose in the share sheet or in Files. The recipient's terms and privacy policy then apply.

## Apple

Apple processes your purchase and download. If you allowed sharing with app developers in iOS Settings (Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements), Apple may give us aggregated crash reports and usage figures that do not identify you. TestFlight testers can also send feedback and screenshots themselves.

## Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will carry a new date, here and in the app.
